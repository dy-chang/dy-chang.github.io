Immersive Virtual Reality Work Zone Inspection Platform

Developed a 3D simulation platform using Unity and Motion Capture to evaluate DOT inspectors' ability to spot temporary traffic control defects. The results demonstrated a jump from a 44% baseline quiz score to a 79% accuracy score in immersive VR, with 97% of inspectors endorsing VR's practical training utility.

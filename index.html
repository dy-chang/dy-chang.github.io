Daeyeol (Daniel) Chang, Ph.D.

Senior Transportation Planner & Modeler • Benesch (Baltimore, MD)

I am a Senior Transportation Planner and Modeler at Benesch with over a decade of experience spanning regional travel demand forecasting, dynamic traffic assignment (DTA), connected vehicle data analytics, and transportation network resilience.

Prior to joining Benesch, I served as a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Morgan State University SMARTER Center and a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Missouri-Columbia. My work focuses on translating multi-source transportation big data (Iteris ClearGuide, INRIX GPS probe data, Smart Card transit records, Census) into practical, defensible modeling and operational frameworks for state DOTs, MPOs, and regional planning authorities.
